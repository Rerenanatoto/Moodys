📐 METODOLOGIA IMPLEMENTADA:
================================================================================

FÓRMULAS DE CÁLCULO:

1. Economic Resiliency
   = (Economic Strength + Institutions & Governance) / 2

2. Government Financial Strength
   = (Economic Resiliency × 0.60) + (Fiscal Strength × 0.40)

3. Scorecard-Indicated Rating
   = GFS + Event Risk Adjustment
   
   Ajuste de Event Risk:
   - High Risk: -2 notches
   - Moderate Risk: -1 notch
   - Low Risk: 0 notches

ESCALA DE RATINGS:
  AAA, AA1, AA2, AA3, A1, A2, A3, BAA1, BAA2, BAA3, BA1, BA2, BA3,
  B1, B2, B3, CAA1, CAA2, CAA3, CA, C
